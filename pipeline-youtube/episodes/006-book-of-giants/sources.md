# 006 The Book of Giants — sources

## Opened and used
- J. R. Davila, "The Book of Giants" (intro + reconstructed narrative), in Bauckham, Davila & Panayotov (eds.), Old Testament Pseudepigrapha: More Noncanonical Scriptures 1 (2013), excerpt: https://bibleinterp.arizona.edu/sites/bibleinterp.arizona.edu/files/2025-04/Davila-BauckhamExcerptGiants_0.pdf — narrative order, dreams, Mahaway's flights, Enoch's tablets, Gilgamesh/Hobabish, Manichaean ending (Ohyah vs Leviathan and Raphael), Gelasian "Ogias" = Ohyah, ~10 Qumran copies, Midrash of Shemhazai and Aza'el.
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, "Giants, Book of": https://www.iranicaonline.org/?p=2673 — Mani's canon (Kephalaia 148, Ibn al-Nadim), languages (Middle Persian, Sogdian, Old Turkic, Coptic), Henning 1934/1943, Sundermann.
- Wikipedia, "The Book of Giants": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Giants — manuscript list (1Q23, 1Q24, 2Q26, 4Q203, 4Q530–532, 4Q556, 4Q206, 6Q8), Milik 1971/1976, Stuckenbruck 1997, dating.
- M. R. James, The Lost Apocrypha of the Old Testament (SPCK 1920), archive.org/details/lostapocryphaofo00jameuoft: p. xiii (Gelasian list, his translation, quoted), p. 40 ("Og", "How we should like to have the book…", guess: Manichaean, Timotheus's list).
- R. H. Charles, The Book of Enoch (SPCK 1917), archive.org/details/book-of-enoch_1917: 1 Enoch 6:6 (200 on Hermon), 15:8 (quoted).
- KJV: Genesis 6:4, Daniel 7:10, Baruch 3:26–28 (checked: https://www.bible-center.fr/fr/bibletext/kjv_eng/bar/3:26-28).

## Facts from general knowledge, cross-checked in the above
- Mani b. 216 in Mesopotamia; Augustine a Manichaean hearer for nine years (Confessions); Uyghur khaganate adopted Manichaeism 762/3.
- German Turfan expeditions 1902–1914 (Grünwedel, Le Coq); Kocho (Chotscho/Gaochang) Manichaean finds; Le Coq, Chotscho (1913).
- Qumran Cave 1 found 1947, Cave 4 in 1952 (~15,000 fragments).
