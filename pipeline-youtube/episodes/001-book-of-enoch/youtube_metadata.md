## Description
There is a book the Bible itself quotes, yet you won't find it in most Bibles. The Book of Enoch tells of two hundred angels called the Watchers, who swore an oath on Mount Hermon; of the giants they fathered; of Azazel and forbidden knowledge; and of the man who "walked with God, and he was not."

We follow the book from the Genesis mystery to the Epistle of Jude, from its disappearance in the West to the monasteries of Ethiopia, and to the Aramaic fragments found among the Dead Sea Scrolls at Qumran.
