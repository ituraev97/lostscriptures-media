# 010 The Ethiopian Bible — sources

## Texts quoted (public domain)
- King James Version: Acts 8:27; Jude 14; Psalm 68:31
- Kebra Nagast ch. 24, tr. E. A. Wallis Budge, *The Queen of Sheba and Her Only Son Menyelek* (London, 1922) — archive.org/details/queenofshebahero00budgrich (also Budge's preface for the 1314–1344 colophon date and Isaac the translator)

## Reference (read, summarised, not quoted)
- Orthodox Tewahedo biblical canon (81 books; narrower vs broader canon; Fetha Negest says 81 but lists 73; Sinodos, Covenant, Clement, Didascalia) — https://cavac.at/cavacopedia/Orthodox%20Tewahedo%20biblical%20canon (Wikipedia mirror)
- Garima Gospels, Oxford radiocarbon 330–650, legend of Abba Garima (494), Temple image, 20+ birds, in-situ conservation by the Ethiopian Heritage Fund — https://www.medievalists.net/2010/07/garima-gospels-found-to-be-oldest-surviving-christian-illustrated-manuscripts/ ; https://www.ethiopianheritagefund.org/completed-project-1-the-garima-gospels-in-context
- Frumentius, pirates/attack, Ezana, Athanasius c. 333 — https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/saint-frumentius/
- James Bruce's three Ethiopic Enoch copies (1773), Laurence 1821, Dillmann 1851/1853 — https://scripturecentral.org/archive/periodicals/magazine-article/strange-thing-land-return-book-enoch-part-2
- Ark at Aksum, Kebra Nagast 14th c., guardian monk, tabot in every church, historians skeptical — https://www.britannica.com/story/where-is-the-ark-of-the-covenant
- 1 Meqabyan "content completely different from 1 Maccabees" — https://tips.translation.bible/story/the-book-of-1-meqabyan/
- General knowledge checked against the above: Ezana's coins with the cross; Mani's four kingdoms; Kush/Candace (Kandake) as the biblical "Ethiopia"; 4 Baruch's Abimelech sleeping 66 years.
