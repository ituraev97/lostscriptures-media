Why does the Ethiopian Bible have 81 books? The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church kept copying books most other churches let go, and the Book of Enoch and the Book of Jubilees survive complete only in Ethiopia's ancient language, Ge'ez.
Inside: the kingdom of Aksum, the 81-book canon (and why even its lists differ), Meqabyan, 4 Baruch, the Garima Gospels and the Kebra Nagast's claim about the Ark of the Covenant.
