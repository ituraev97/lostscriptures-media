# Sources — 002 The Book of Jubilees

## Texts quoted on screen (public domain)
- R. H. Charles, The Book of Jubilees or the Little Genesis, SPCK 1917 (reprint of the 1902 translation) — archive.org/details/bookofjubileesor01char. Quotes: Prologue; 1:27; 3:28; 10:8; 6:32.

## Facts checked against
- J. C. VanderKam, "Jubilees, Book of", Anchor Bible Dictionary (1992) — names (Little Genesis, Divisions of the Times), Ethiopic only complete version, Qumran copies, date c. 170–140 BC, angel of the presence, 364-day year, heavenly tablets, Noah/Abraham festivals, Levi.
- Britannica, "Book of Jubilees" — 49-year jubilees, Qumran / Damascus Document, Ethiopic from Greek from Hebrew.
- BAS Library, "Torah Before Sinai" — at least 15 copies at Qumran.
- New World Encyclopedia — ~15 scrolls in five caves; only Psalms, Deuteronomy, Isaiah, Exodus, Genesis better represented; Ethiopian canon "Mets'hafe Kufale".
- Jubilees Palimpsest Project (St. Mary's University) — Latin copied 5th c., erased 8th c. (Bobbio), Ceriani editio princeps 1861.
- Charles 1917 introduction — Dillmann Ethiopic text 1859; Charles Ethiopic edition 1895; Greek citations (Justin, Origen, Epiphanius, Syncellus); author rejects the lunar calendar.
- Jubilees 50:4 (Charles) — 49 jubilees + 1 week + 2 years to Moses (2410 A.M.) + 40 years = 2450 = 50 jubilees.
- Jubilees 3:15–17 (seven years in Eden), 4:9 (Awan), 4:31 (Cain's death), 4:17 (Enoch and writing), 5:28 (Lubar), 10:1–14 (Mastema, one tenth, Noah's book of medicines), 11:11–21 (ravens), 12:12–14 (burning of the idols, Haran).
