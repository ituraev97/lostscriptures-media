A book that claims an angel dictated it to Moses on Mount Sinai. It retells Genesis with seven years in Eden, animals that could speak, a demon prince named Mastema, and a sacred year of exactly 364 days.

We follow the Book of Jubilees, the "Little Genesis," from its jubilee clock of 49-year cycles to the Ethiopian church that kept it whole, a scraped-off Latin palimpsest in Milan, and the Hebrew copies found among the Dead Sea Scrolls at Qumran.
