The Bible says no hammer or axe was heard while Solomon's Temple was being built. The Testament of Solomon, a Greek book from the early Christian centuries, tells why: an archangel gives the king a ring, and with it Solomon forces demons to cut the stones.

We follow the story from the boy robbed by the demon Ornias to the ring from the archangel Michael, the interview with the prince of demons, the wind of Arabia caught in a leather flask, the cornerstone and the pillar from the Red Sea, and Solomon's fall for five locusts. Then the evidence: Josephus, McCown's 1922 edition, and the ten ancient rings inscribed "Solomon says: watch out!"
