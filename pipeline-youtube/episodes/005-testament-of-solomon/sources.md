# 005 The Testament of Solomon — sources

## Text quoted on screen (public domain)
- F. C. Conybeare, "The Testament of Solomon", Jewish Quarterly Review 11 (October 1898), pp. 1–45, translated from Fleck's Greek text (1837). archive.org `jstor-1450398` (djvu text used for every quote: §5 the ring, §114 falling stars, §130 the confession). Introduction: Fleck 1837, Bornemann 1844; pentalpha = five A's interlaced; Obizuth ≈ Medusa; thirty-six decani.
- Josephus, Antiquities 8.2.5, tr. W. Whiston (1737), text from `worksofflaviusjo1890jose` (Eleazar before Vespasian, ring with a root, incantations of Solomon).

## Facts and dating
- C. C. McCown, The Testament of Solomon (Leipzig 1922), `cu31924028957400`: ch. IX — terminus a quo 100, ad quem c. 400 (Dialogue of Timothy and Aquila); conclusion "written early in the third century"; Jewish groundwork (d) may be first century; Rec. B 4th–5th c., Rec. C 12th–13th c.; manuscripts D (Athos, 16th c.), H (Holkham, 15th c.), I (Paris suppl. gr. 500, 16th c.) etc.
- Gradel & Pedersen, The Lost Novel of King Solomon and the Demons (Mohr Siebeck, online 26 Nov 2025): ten 3rd-century rings inscribed "Solomon says: 'Watch out!'"; argue a 2nd-century date for the original and a longer third-person narrative; Alexandria plausible. Publisher page + PaleoJudaica (Jan 2026).
- Encyclopaedia Judaica via encyclopedia.com: Greek, c. 100–300 CE, Josephus Ant. 8:45ff, Git. 68, five locusts to Moloch, Christian elements.
- Early Jewish Writings (Charlesworth/Duling summary): McCown early 3rd c. with 1st-c. Jewish composition; Christian passages §§54, 65, 122.
- Calvin University news: Greek text, 1st–3rd c. by style; McCown 1922 the last full study.
- KJV: 1 Kings 6:7 (no hammer nor axe), 1 Kings 11:4 (wives turned away his heart), Psalm 118:22.
