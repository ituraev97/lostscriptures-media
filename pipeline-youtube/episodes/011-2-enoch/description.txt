What did Enoch see in the 10 heavens? Second Enoch (2 Enoch), also called the Book of the Secrets of Enoch or Slavonic Enoch, follows Enoch from his bed through heaven after heaven to the face of God.
The twist: the famous eighth, ninth and tenth heavens appear in only one family of manuscripts, and the rest of the book speaks of seven. We read the 1896 English translation, trace how the Slavonic manuscripts survived, and look at the Coptic pages found in Nubia.
