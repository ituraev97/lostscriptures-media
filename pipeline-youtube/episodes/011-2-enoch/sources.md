# 011 — 2 Enoch: sources

## Texts quoted on screen (public domain)
- W. R. Morfill (tr.), R. H. Charles (ed.), *The Book of the Secrets of Enoch* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1896) — https://archive.org/details/cu31924014633568 — quotes: 1:4-5; 7:5 (Charles's verse 4-5); 22:1 long version (A, printed in Charles's notes); 22:10; 58:4. Facts: intro pp. xiii–xvi (MSS A = Khludov MS published by Popov 1880; Sokolov's Belgrade MS 1886; B short recension; interpolation of 8th–10th heavens in A, xx. 3, xxi–xxii. 3), pp. xxvi (date 1–50 AD, Alexandria, temple standing), xxvii (world-week of 7000 years), note on lviii (animal souls); Appendix (Melchizedek fragment, Sopanima, Michael takes the child to Eden).
- King James Version: Genesis 5:24; 2 Corinthians 12:2-4.

## Reference
- Wikipedia, "2 Enoch" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_Enoch (more than 20 Slavonic MSS and fragments, 14th–18th c.; long/short recensions; Coptic fragments of chs. 36–42 identified 2009; dates proposed from 1st c. BC to 10th c. AD).
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Second Book of Enoch" — https://www.britannica.com/topic/Second-Book-of-Enoch (surviving form possibly a 7th-c. Christian reworking of a 1st-c. Jewish text, Alexandria).
- Antiquitopia (2009), "2 Enoch in Coptic" — https://antiquitopia.blogspot.com/2009/04/2-enoch-in-coptic.html (Joost Hagen, Leiden; Qasr Ibrim, excavated 1972 by the Egypt Exploration Society; four leaves, chs. 36–42, short recension; palaeography 8th–9th, possibly 10th c.).
- A. Orlov & G. Boccaccini (eds.), *New Perspectives on 2 Enoch: No Longer Slavonic Only* (Brill, 2012) — https://epublications.marquette.edu/marq_fac-book/350
