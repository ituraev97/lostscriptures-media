## Description
Fall asleep to the Book of Enoch. Tonight we read the Book of the Watchers (1 Enoch, chapters 1–36) slowly and calmly over a soft, quiet background: the two hundred angels on Mount Hermon, Enoch's vision of the throne of God, and his journeys to the ends of the earth.

Dim the lights, put your phone down and let the ancient words carry you into rest. The screen grows darker in the last ten minutes.
